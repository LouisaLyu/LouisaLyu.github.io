title: Design philosophy

I believe design is about understanding people’s needs and finding effective ways to address them. Interaction design, specifically, should focus less on visual extravagance and more on the functionality of the product, as well as the range of audiences it can serve. While visual appeal is important, how users feel, how they interact, and what they gain from it should be at the core of the design.

My internships and everyday life have shown me that design requires an ongoing effort to understand different people. Designers should not dictate a single correct way for others to interpret or experience a design. Instead, they should give people the freedom to find their own way through it. As such, I value research and experimentation because I want to understand why people respond differently to the same design. I am particularly interested in creating immersive environments that combine visual, physical, and audio elements and invite people to explore and respond.

I want to make things that matter to other people while continuing to learn from them. Interaction design gives me a way to express ideas, understand different perspectives, and see how my work affects others. I intend to pursue a career in immersive interaction design, where I can help develop interactive installations that let people shape their own experiences through their actions and the surrounding environment.
